    How a signal fires

    At every bar close the engine scores a long case and a short case out of 100. A signal fires only when all of these hold:

    • Price is at an edge (location points above zero).
    • A trigger printed on this bar: a fractal sweep and reclaim, or a structure shift.
    • TP1 pays at least 1.0R and the stop is no wider than 4× ATR.
    • The score clears the threshold you set.

    Factors without data (the order book on OHLCV files) drop out and the score is rescaled.

    Location 25

    Fixed-range volume profile over the lookback window: POC, and a 70% value area grown out from the POC. Full points when price tests VAL (longs) or VAH (shorts), or stretches to the VWAP ±2σ band. 18 for ±1σ. 14 for a pullback that holds the POC or VWAP inside a higher-timeframe trend.

    Trigger 20

    Williams fractals (5 bars, confirmed two bars later, so they never repaint) mark swing liquidity on the base and higher timeframes. 20 for sweeping a higher-timeframe fractal and closing back inside, 17 for a base fractal, 15 for a change of character.

    Switch the entry trigger to Order flow only to drop fractals: the engine then enters at the edge when absorption or delta divergence prints, and the stop goes beyond the bar extreme. Changing the trigger or the HTF switch replays the whole history so the journals are comparable.

    Order book 15

    A wall is a level with at least 3× the median resting size. 10 when a wall on your side has rested for 6 of the last 10 bars, 4 when it is fresh and could be a spoof. 5 more when no opposite wall of 4× median sits between entry and TP1.

    Order flow 15

    15 for absorption: at least 55% aggressive volume against you, yet the bar closes in your half. 10 for delta divergence at a new extreme. 8 for a delta flip. On OHLCV files, a volume spike with a rejection close stands in.

    Structure and room 15 + 10

    15 when the higher-timeframe fractal structure (last break of a swing) agrees, 7 when it is undecided. Room scores 10 at 1.5R or more to TP1, 6 at 1.0R.

    Trade management

    Stop goes beyond the swept level or the bar extreme. It is never closer than 0.8× the 1m ATR, 0.6× the 15m ATR, or 8× your trading costs, whichever is widest, so normal gold noise and spread cannot take it out. TP1 is the nearer of POC and VWAP; TP2 is the far value edge, a σ band or an untaken swing. Half is banked at TP1 and the stop moves to breakeven. Trades time out after 240 bars. If a bar touches both stop and target, the journal counts the stop.

    Pips on gold: 1 pip = $0.10. Losses are counted in pips too, so net pips is real.
