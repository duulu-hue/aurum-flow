    Your FRVP model

    The profile runs from the start of the previous trading day to now. A short needs all of: price trades up into the POC, a resistance HVN or VAH and closes back below it; buy-side liquidity was taken in the last 16 bars (previous day high, or the high of one of the last two higher-timeframe candles) and price is back below it; a bearish CISD on the entry timeframe (a close below the open of the last run of up-candles); and no chop (fewer than 5 POC crosses in 16 bars). Longs are the mirror at the POC, a support HVN or VAL after sell-side liquidity is taken.

    The stop sits beyond the swept extreme and the zone, at least 0.4× and at most 1.5× a higher-timeframe candle. TP1 is the first key level at least 1R away (HVN, VAL/VAH, POC, previous day high/low, profile extreme); TP2 is the farthest one that pays at least your minimum (2.5R by default). Half is banked at TP1, the stop goes to breakeven, and trades time out after 24 hours. Use 15-minute entries to match your chart; the higher timeframe is then 4H.

    How a signal fires (value-edge engine)

    At every bar close the engine scores a long case and a short case out of 100. A signal fires only when all of these hold:

    • Price is at an edge (location points above zero).
    • A trigger printed on this bar: a fractal sweep and reclaim, or a structure shift.
    • TP1 pays at least 1.0R and the stop is no wider than 4× ATR.
    • The score clears the threshold you set.

    Factors without data (the order book on OHLCV files) drop out and the score is rescaled.

    Location 25

    Value comes from your Fixed Range Volume Profile Pro: the range is split into rows (24 by default), each candle's volume goes into the row at its high/low midpoint, the POC is the busiest row, and the value area grows one row at a time from the POC toward the bigger neighbour until it holds 70% of the volume. The engine reads it over a rolling window, today's session or the previous session (FRVP settings).

    Full points when price tests VAL (longs) or VAH (shorts), or stretches to the VWAP ±2σ band. 20 when a bar dips into a high-volume node (HVN) and closes back past its middle. 18 for VWAP ±1σ. 14 for a pullback that holds the POC or VWAP with the trend. HVNs are also used as targets. Forex and synthetics have no feed volume, so their profile counts time at price.

    Trigger 20

    Williams fractals (5 bars, confirmed two bars later, so they never repaint) mark swing liquidity on the base and higher timeframes. 20 for sweeping a higher-timeframe fractal and closing back inside, 17 for a base fractal, 15 for a change of character.

    Switch the entry trigger to Order flow only to drop fractals: the engine then enters at the edge when absorption or delta divergence prints, and the stop goes beyond the bar extreme. Changing the trigger or the HTF switch replays the whole history so the journals are comparable.

    Order book 15

    A wall is a level with at least 3× the median resting size. 10 when a wall on your side has rested for 6 of the last 10 bars, 4 when it is fresh and could be a spoof. 5 more when no opposite wall of 4× median sits between entry and TP1.

    Order flow 15

    15 for absorption: at least 55% aggressive volume against you, yet the bar closes in your half. 10 for delta divergence at a new extreme. 8 for a delta flip. On OHLCV files, a volume spike with a rejection close stands in.

    Structure and room 15 + 10

    15 when the higher-timeframe fractal structure (last break of a swing) agrees, 7 when it is undecided. Room scores 10 at 1.5R or more to TP1, 6 at 1.0R.

    Day type 15

    The engine first asks what kind of day it is. If at least 75% of the last 20 bars closed above a rising VWAP, it is a trend day up: sells against it are blocked, and buys on pullbacks to VWAP, the POC or the value-area low are allowed. A trend day down is the mirror. Anything else is a balanced day, where fading the value-area edges is allowed.

    Trade management

    A fade needs a failed auction: price trades outside value (or beyond a VWAP band) on this bar or the last one and this bar closes back inside. A close that stays outside value is acceptance, and the engine will not fade it.

    The stop goes beyond the probe, never closer than one higher-timeframe candle (1H for 3m and 5m entries) or 8× your costs. TP1 is the nearer of POC and VWAP; TP2 is the far value edge, a σ band, an HVN or an untaken swing. Half is banked at TP1 and the stop moves to breakeven. Trades time out after about four hours.

    Forex and metals only signal during your trading hours. Each market holds one signal at a time, and a new signal is skipped if it would double a currency exposure an open signal already has (selling EURUSD and GBPUSD together is buying the dollar twice). The chart draws exactly the signals in your journal.
